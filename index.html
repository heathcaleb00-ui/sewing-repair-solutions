Our services

Clean, lubricate, adjust, repair. All brands and models.

Every machine is taken apart, cleaned, set back to factory specs, and tested before it comes home.

TAG 01 · HOME SEWING

Sewing Machines

  • Total disassembly of every machine
  • Motors, gears, and sensors thoroughly cleaned
  • Everything adjusted to factory specs, including those pesky, temperamental needle threaders
  • Timing and tension adjusted as needed
  • New needle, free of charge
  • Every single stitch test-sewn

Your "Baby" comes home looking and sewing as perfectly as the day you brought her home.

TAG 02 · OVERLOCK

Sergers

  • All covers removed to reach the gears inside
  • Lint and debris thoroughly cleaned out
  • Loopers and tension discs adjusted as needed
  • All moving parts oiled and greased
  • New needles in every machine
  • Completely checked to make sure it sews properly

We leave it fully threaded. We know how hard those loopers are to thread, so you can start sewing right away.

TAG 03 · EMBROIDERY & COMBO

Embroidery Machines

  • Complete breakdown of the main machine and the embroidery attachment
  • Gears and moving parts cleaned on both
  • Adjusted to factory specs and carefully reassembled
  • A variety of stitches sewn out
  • A complicated multi-thread design stitched on your embroidery unit

These machines have numerous sensors and motors. Nothing leaves our shop without being fully tested.
